Spain's business-to-business electronic invoicing regime now has a firm timetable. Order HAC/1028/2026 was published in the Official State Gazette on 5 October and entered into force on 6 October. That starts the countdown: 6 October 2027 for businesses and professionals with annual turnover above €8 million, and 6 October 2028 for everyone else in scope.

On the same day, the Ministry of Finance announced that it **plans** to move the remaining computerised invoicing system requirements, commonly referred to as VERI*FACTU, to October 2028. The distinction matters. This is an official intention, but it is not yet the law in force: the relevant amendment still has to be approved and published.

The practical answer is therefore twofold. The B2B timetable is confirmed; the new VERI*FACTU date remains planned. This article provides general operational information, not tax or legal advice for a particular business.

## What changed this week

[Law 18/2022 on business creation and growth](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2022-15818) introduced mandatory e-invoicing between businesses and professionals in Spain. [Royal Decree 238/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-7295), published in March, provided the operating rules, but tied the transition periods to a further order governing the public e-invoicing service.

That final piece has now been published. [Order HAC/1028/2026](https://www.boe.es/eli/es/o/2026/10/02/hac1028/con), published on 5 October 2026 and effective the following day, regulates the public platform and starts the statutory clock.

## B2B e-invoicing and VERI*FACTU are not the same project

They are easily confused because both concern invoices, but they create different requirements.

**B2B e-invoicing** governs how invoices are issued, received and exchanged between businesses and professionals. An invoice must use a structured format — such as UBL, Facturae, CII or EDIFACT — so that its data can be processed automatically. Emailing a PDF is not enough on its own. Recipients will also have to report statuses such as acceptance or rejection and full payment under [Royal Decree 238/2026](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2026-7295).

The **Regulation on computerised invoicing system requirements**, or RRSIF, sets rules for the software that creates invoice records. Strictly speaking, VERI*FACTU is one of the two permitted modes: it sends invoicing records to the Spanish Tax Agency as they are created. The alternative keeps them within the business's own system under enhanced integrity, traceability and control requirements.

A business may need to prepare for both. Replacing the program that issues invoices does not automatically provide structured B2B exchange and status management. Likewise, connecting to a B2B platform does not by itself prove that the issuing system complies with the RRSIF.

## The B2B dates businesses can now plan around

The transition period depends on the previous year's net turnover:

- **Above €8 million:** the obligation starts on 6 October 2027, twelve months after the order entered into force.
- **Up to €8 million:** the obligation starts on 6 October 2028, twenty-four months after entry into force.

For transactions in scope, issuers and recipients must be able to work through a private platform or the public service. If the recipient has not specified a private reception point, the public service becomes the default. Private platforms must send a faithful copy to the public infrastructure, as required by the royal decree and the order.

There are exceptions, including certain simplified invoices. Before buying or configuring a tool, confirm the actual scope with your accountant or legal adviser and the software provider: transaction types, where recipients are established, turnover and every system involved.

## Where VERI*FACTU stands today

The Ministry of Finance's [information note dated 5 October 2026](https://www.hacienda.gob.es/sgt/gabsehacienda/nota-informativa-verifactu.pdf) says that the government plans to amend the rules so the outstanding obligations fall in October 2028. The stated aim is to align invoicing software and B2B e-invoicing timetables.

Until that amendment is approved and published in the Official State Gazette, the [Spanish Tax Agency's current guidance](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu/nota-informativa-ampliacion-plazo-adaptacion-facturacion.html) still gives 1 January 2027 for Corporate Income Tax taxpayers and 1 July 2027 for other affected taxpayers.

The announcement should inform planning, but it should not be treated as enacted legislation. Avoid both rushing into a purchase because of a deadline likely to move and freezing the entire project as if the delay were final. Ask the provider to distinguish what works today, what depends on an update and what would change if the October 2028 date is confirmed in the Gazette.

## A practical plan that avoids doing the work twice

1. **Map the issuing workflow.** Record which program creates the invoice, who reviews it, how it is sent and where its records are kept.
2. **Separate sales and purchase invoices.** The processes are related, but their owners, data and tools are different.
3. **Confirm scope using real data.** Check last year's turnover, invoice types and whether customers act as businesses or professionals established in Spain.
4. **Ask about both regimes.** The provider should explain RRSIF compliance and B2B e-invoicing connectivity separately.
5. **Test formats and statuses.** Ask which structured formats it supports, how it receives acceptance or rejection and how payment is reported.
6. **Keep a data exit route.** Define how invoices, records and statuses can be exported if you change platform.
7. **Follow the Gazette, not only sales announcements.** The Tax Agency's [computerised invoicing systems hub](https://www3.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/iva/sistemas-informaticos-facturacion-verifactu.html) brings together official RRSIF information.
