On 19 November, OpenAI updated its **Businesses in ChatGPT** announcement to say that ecommerce and online service businesses outside the United States can now be eligible to claim their profile and publish a Business AI Agent in ChatGPT. This matters to SMEs in Europe, but it is not automatic access for every company: the business and its representative must be verified, category requirements apply, and the option must actually be available in Business Hub.

## What changed

OpenAI launched the beta on 10 November 2026. The [announcement was updated on 19 November](https://openai.com/index/introducing-businesses-chatgpt/) to say that ecommerce and online service businesses based outside the US can claim their business and publish agents in ChatGPT.

Businesses in ChatGPT has three separate parts:

1. **Claimed profile:** proves that you represent the business and lets you update its website, description, category, logo and delivery or return policies.
2. **Information and insights:** provides aggregate signals such as mentions, website clicks and discovery topics.
3. **Business Agent:** an optional AI agent that answers questions about products, services and policies using information supplied by the company.

Claiming a profile does not require publishing an agent or advertising. It does not guarantee more visibility, a higher position or sales either. The [OpenAI Help Center](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt) says ChatGPT's independent recommendations remain separate.

## Availability still needs to be confirmed in the account

The official documentation is not fully aligned yet. The 19 November update says eligible businesses outside the US can publish agents. The Help Center also describes eligible ecommerce and online service businesses without explicitly restricting them to the US, while noting that availability depends on location, business type, industry and verification. At the same time, the [product page FAQ](https://openai.com/business/businesses-in-chatgpt/) still says Business Agents are available in the United States.

The cautious reading is that expansion has started, but rollout and documentation updates are gradual. For a business in Europe, the decisive evidence is not the headline. It is whether Business Hub allows the profile to be claimed and, after approval, presents the option to create an agent.

If the option is absent, do not work around the restriction or misstate the business location. The company can prepare its information and wait until its account and category become eligible.

## What verification requires

According to the [official setup guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001635-setting-up-your-business-on-chatgpt), only an owner or authorised representative may claim a business. Depending on the case, verification may be offered through a phone number, business email, website domain or Shopify.

After proving the connection to the company, the representative must complete identity verification with Persona. OpenAI may request a government ID, business registration details and proof of authority. Persona approval is required before publishing profile changes, viewing insights, connecting a product feed or creating an eligible agent.

Prepare:

- the legal business name and trading name as separate fields;
- an address and registration details that match official records;
- access to the domain or corporate email;
- internal authorisation if the administrator is not the owner;
- a public website with current offerings, contact details and policies.

A verified profile does not guarantee agent eligibility. They are separate decisions.

## What a Business Agent can do today

The agent helps a person explore the company's offering in a conversation clearly identified as the business's agent. It can explain products, services, differences, stated availability and policies based on supplied information.

At this stage it is not a complete operational agent. The Help Center says it **cannot complete purchases, make bookings or check a customer's order**. It can explain and link to the website, where the customer completes the action. If the company also has a plugin, the Business Agent's separate chat does not automatically use that plugin's data or capabilities.

That distinction matters. A Business Agent is mainly a discovery and informational service channel inside ChatGPT. An [AI agent connected to a small business's tools](/en/blog/ai-agents-small-business-act-across-tools/) can query systems, propose changes or carry out steps under controlled permissions. They are not the same product and do not solve the same problem.

## What information the business receives

Business Hub provides aggregate activity for the previous 28 days, including mentions, website clicks and discovery topics. OpenAI says it does not give the company individual conversations, customer prompts or Business Agent chats. A blank metric does not necessarily mean zero activity; the privacy threshold may not have been reached or reporting may not yet be available.

These signals can guide content and reveal recurring themes, but they are not a CRM or a lead list. [AI lead qualification](/en/blog/ai-lead-qualification-automate-inbound-enquiries/) requires a separate workflow with consent, minimum necessary data and controlled integrations.

## Boundaries the agent must respect

OpenAI's [Business Agents Policy, updated on 10 November](https://openai.com/policies/business-agents-policy/), provides practical constraints for the agent:

- it must be clear that this is AI representing the business, not a person or independent adviser;
- it must not invent offerings, prices, availability, credentials, reviews, guarantees or results;
- it must not hide relevant fees, conditions or limitations;
- it must not pressure people, create false urgency or keep pushing after a refusal;
- it must not request unnecessary or high-risk personal data;
- it must not provide advice that requires a professional licence or automate high-impact decisions without human review.

The [Businesses in ChatGPT Terms](https://openai.com/policies/businesses-in-chatgpt-terms/) make the company responsible for the agent's information, configuration, safety testing and error correction. Publishing does not transfer that responsibility to the provider.

## Pre-publication checklist

### 1. Give it a narrow role

A reasonable first scope is answering questions about services, published prices, coverage, indicative timelines and policies. Do not turn it into an adviser, autonomous quoting tool or channel for sensitive complaints.

### 2. Clean the source material

Review the website, catalogue, descriptions and policies. Remove expired prices, vague promises and contradictions between pages. If you sell products, the feed needs current prices and availability.

### 3. Write explicit boundaries

Define what it may say, when it should link to the website, when it must acknowledge uncertainty and which requests are out of scope. “I don't have that information” is better than an invented commercial answer.

### 4. Test realistic and adversarial questions

Include comparisons, returns, non-existent discounts, result guarantees, personal-data requests and attempts to override instructions. The [AI assistant testing checklist](/en/blog/ai-assistant-testing-checklist-before-production/) covers sources, permissions, prompt injection, failures and escalation.

### 5. Assign maintenance

Someone must own changes to prices, offerings, policies and problematic answers. OpenAI reviews updates and they can take time to appear. Saving a change does not mean every answer will reflect it immediately.

## What an SME should do now

If Business Hub is available in the account, claiming the profile, completing verification and correcting basic details is worthwhile even when the agent option is absent. It is reversible and helps the company manage the information it supplies.

Publish the agent only when the offering is documented, questions repeat and a named person will test and maintain it. If the business needs bookings, order lookups, tailored quotations or updates to internal systems, it should assess its own assistant or automation. The current beta does not replace those integrations.
