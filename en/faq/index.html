GENERAL

About VBG Labs

Who are you and where are you based?

We're VBG Labs, a technology consultancy based in Catalonia, Spain. We work with businesses across Spain and beyond, in person or remote.

Do I need to know about AI or tech to work with you?

No. We speak your language, not ours. We handle the technical side.

What languages do you work in?

Catalan, Spanish and English, both in day-to-day communication and in the documents we deliver.

Do you only work with large companies?

No, quite the opposite: we mostly work with small and medium businesses. That's our core audience.

SERVICES AND PRICING

How we work, and what it costs

Where should I start?

If you're not sure, start with consulting (350–650€ + VAT). It gives you a diagnosis and a roadmap before you spend anything on implementation.

Are the prices you publish final?

They're indicative, to give you a sense of scale. The final quote depends on the specific scope of your case, and we always confirm it in writing before starting.

Do you always end up recommending automating everything?

No. Sometimes the best advice is to leave things alone, or just tidy up what you already have.

How long does a typical project take?

A one-off automation usually takes 1-3 weeks. Consulting, 1-2 weeks. Custom software projects are scoped case by case within the roadmap.

Do you offer ongoing support after implementation?

Yes, as optional ongoing support, from 120€/mo + VAT.

DATA AND PRIVACY

How we handle information

What happens to my business's data?

It's used exclusively for the project you shared it for, and never shared with third parties without your consent. More detail in our privacy policy.

Does VBG Invoicing share data between customers?

No. Every account is independent — one customer's data is never visible to another.

Can I ask you to delete my data?

Yes, at any time. Email info@vbglabs.com with the subject "Data rights request".
