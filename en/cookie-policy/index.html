Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites store on your device when you visit them. They're used to remember preferences and, where applicable, gather anonymous statistics about site usage.

2. Cookies used on this site

Cookie or storageTypePurposeDuration
vbg-cookie-consentEssential (localStorage)Stores whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookiesUp to 24 months
_gaAnalytics, set by GoogleDistinguishes users and produces usage statisticsUp to 2 years
_ga_6D4DW5CPW9Analytics, set by GoogleMaintains the measurement session stateUp to 2 years

The _ga cookies are only created if you select “Accept”. If you reject them, the Google Analytics tag is not downloaded and no measurement data is sent.

3. Types of cookies

Essential cookies

Required for the site's basic operation. These don't require consent.

Analytics cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand, in aggregate, which pages are visited, which channels bring visitors to the site and how the site is used. Advertising signals remain disabled, and Analytics is only loaded after you give consent.

The provider is Google Ireland Limited. See Google's Privacy Policy for information about its processing.

Advertising cookies

This site does not use advertising cookies.

4. Managing cookies

You can accept, reject or withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing consent blocks Analytics and removes the analytics cookies accessible from this site.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

5. Updates to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect regulatory changes or changes to the services used by the website.
