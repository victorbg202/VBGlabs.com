THE PROBLEM

Having access to AI isn't the same as knowing how to use it

Plenty of companies already give their teams access to ChatGPT, Copilot or similar tools, but nobody has actually taught them how to use them well: when it's useful, when not to trust it blindly, and what information should never go into it. The result is usually shallow use — or, worse, risky use.

Our training isn't a general talk about "what is AI." It's hands-on, applied to your team's real tasks, and always includes a section on limits and data privacy.

WHAT WE COVER
