Questions before getting started

Is this viable for a small business?

It depends on task duration, frequency and implementation and running costs. We assess those figures first: a template or process change may be enough.

Do you only work with businesses in Lleida?

We are based in Almenar and prioritise proximity to Lleida and the Segrià area. We also serve businesses across Spain, agreeing remote or in-person work according to the project.

Which costs continue after implementation?

Depending on the workflow, there may be AI usage, hosting, licence or messaging costs. VBG Labs manages and bills these separately, estimating them before agreement. Maintenance is agreed for the project.

Can I commission just the assessment?

Yes. AI and automation consulting can assess the workflow and provide a roadmap without committing you to implementation. If the scope is already clear, we can assess the project directly.
