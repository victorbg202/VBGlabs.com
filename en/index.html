HOW WE WORK

Five steps, no surprises

The same method whether you hire just a consulting session or we end up building everything.

  1. 01

    Understand how you work

    We talk to the people doing the work day to day, not only whoever manages it.

  2. 02

    Spot problems and opportunities

    We find where time is lost, where errors are likely, and where the real room for improvement is.

  3. 03

    Prioritize

    Not everything is worth automating. We rank by real impact against the effort it takes.

  4. 04

    Set a roadmap

    A concrete plan, with phases and an indicative budget, so you can decide with real information.

  5. 05

    Implement (if it makes sense)

    If you decide to go ahead, we build, test with your team, and train whoever will use it.
