CONSULTING

An outside view before you decide anything

We analyze how your business runs today and give you a clear roadmap of what's worth changing first. It stands on its own — there's no obligation to have us implement anything afterwards.

WHO IT'S FOR

When consulting makes sense

If you sense the business could run more smoothly but aren't sure where to start, or want an independent technical opinion before committing to a large project, consulting is the safest place to begin. We don't propose anything before understanding how your team actually works — not from a form, but by talking to the people doing the work every day.

METHODOLOGY

How we work

  1. 01

    Understand how you work

    Short sessions with the people doing the work day to day, not only whoever manages it.

  2. 02

    Spot problems and opportunities

    We find where time is lost, where errors are likely, and where the real room for improvement is.

  3. 03

    Prioritize

    Not everything is worth changing. We rank by real impact against the effort it takes.

  4. 04

    Set a roadmap

    A concrete document, with phases and an indicative budget for each, so you can decide with real information.

  5. 05

    Implement, if you'd like us to

    If you decide to go ahead, we can build it, test it with your team, and train everyone on it.

WHAT YOU GET

What comes out of a consulting engagement

A written diagnosis

Where time and money are being lost today, with concrete examples from your business.

Clear priorities

What's worth tackling first and what can wait, ranked by real impact.

A priced roadmap

Concrete phases with an indicative budget for each, no surprises.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does consulting commit me to hiring you afterwards?

No. You can keep just the diagnosis and roadmap, and decide for yourself how to proceed.

How long does a consulting engagement take?

Usually 1 to 2 weeks from the first conversations to having the roadmap in hand.

Do I need to prepare anything beforehand?

No. We bring the questions — you just need to let us see how your team actually works.

Do you do consulting for one specific problem, not the whole business?

Yes. Many engagements focus on one area — invoicing, customer service, and so on — rather than the whole business.
