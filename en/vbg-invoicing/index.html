WHAT IT DOES (AND DOESN'T)

It manages the invoices you receive, not the ones you issue

VBG Invoicing focuses on one specific problem: the invoices coming into your business from suppliers and contractors. It centralizes them, reviews them, and gets your tax reports ready. Right now it doesn't issue sales invoices to your own customers — you'll keep doing that with whatever you already use, while VBG Invoicing handles the received side.

Receive and organize

Every invoice you receive, registered on its own

It arrives by email, gets sent to you, or you upload it yourself: VBG Invoicing reads it, pulls out the data, and files it. Nothing gets lost in an inbox.

QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

Does VBG Invoicing issue invoices to my customers?

Not right now. It's focused solely on managing the invoices you receive from suppliers and contractors.

Where is my data stored?

Invoices and related data are stored privately, tied only to your account. We never share data between different customer accounts.

Can I try it before committing?

Yes. Request a demo and we'll walk through it with your real case before you decide.

What happens if I go over my plan's invoice limit?

We'll let you know before you hit it so you can move up a tier without any interruption.

Does it work for accounting firms managing several clients?

Yes — the Business plan includes multi-company management built for exactly that.
