ABOUT US

A digital lab, not another agency

VBG Labs started with a simple idea: technology is only worth it if it makes a specific business run better. Not the other way around.

WHY WE EXIST

Too often, technology gets sold before anyone understands the problem

We kept seeing the same pattern: small and mid-sized businesses that someone had tried to sell a trendy tool to, without ever stopping to understand how they actually worked. The result is usually the same — an investment that never gets used, or that solves a problem that wasn't the priority in the first place.

VBG Labs exists to do it the other way round: understand the business first, then decide whether it's worth automating, applying AI, training the team, or simply tidying up what's already there. Technology is a means, not the starting point.

HOW WE WORK

Our principles

We understand before we propose

No recommendation comes from a template. We always start from how your team actually works.

Technology independence

We're not tied to any one tool or platform. We choose based on the problem.

Honesty over selling

If the best answer is to do nothing, we'll tell you — even if you're willing to pay for it.

We can actually build it

We don't just advise: we build too. VBG Invoicing is the proof.

WHERE WE'RE BASED

Based in Catalonia, working further afield

VBG Labs is based in Catalonia, Spain, and grew up working with small and mid-sized local businesses — shops, workshops, professional practices. That grounding shapes how we work: practical, close to the people actually doing the work. We take on projects across Spain and beyond, in person or remote depending on what makes sense for the work.

OUR OWN PRODUCTS

We don't just say it — we build it

VBG Invoicing is already a real, working product. Postcraft is the second, currently in development. Both prove the same thing: we can take an idea all the way to a working product.
